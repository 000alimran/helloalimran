Krishijaat

A venture that connects the realities of business operations with food and agricultural products.

Overview

Krishijaat is a consumer and agri-food venture involving products such as honey, fish, oils and other food items. It offers a very different perspective from digital-only businesses.

The question behind it

What does it take to translate a promising consumer idea into sourcing, operations, trust, distribution and a sustainable experience?

My relationship to the work
