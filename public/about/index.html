THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Curious by practice.
Intentional by design.

My career has crossed visual communication, technology and entrepreneurship. I see them as connected ways of understanding people and solving problems.

I studied fine arts with a focus on graphic design at the University of Dhaka. My early professional work in advertising and visual communication sparked an interest in how design shapes perception, trust, and everyday decisions.

Over time, that interest moved toward user experience, digital products, and designing not just what people see, but what they can understand and do. I later completed postgraduate studies in information technology while continuing to learn through practical work.

I founded The YOLO Studio to create an environment where strategy, design and technology could meet. Building a company added lessons that classrooms rarely teach: navigating uncertainty, delivering under constraints, and taking responsibility for outcomes.

Today I am exploring more structured research, particularly in human-computer interaction, digital accessibility, and the adoption of emerging technology in Bangladesh and similar contexts.

What I believe

Good design should make a difference to the people who use it, not only to the people who present it.

My work is still evolving. I prefer to show real practice, ongoing questions, and honest limitations rather than exaggerate credentials or outcomes.
