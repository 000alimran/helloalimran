01 / ABOUT

A career shaped by curiosity, rather than a single title.

I started in visual communication and advertising. That work led me to user experience, digital products, entrepreneurship, and a growing interest in research. Each chapter taught me a different way of asking better questions.

I founded The YOLO Studio to connect creativity with execution. Alongside my professional practice, I continue exploring how thoughtful design can make digital services clearer and more useful.
