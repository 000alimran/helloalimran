01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity is the thread that connects my work.

My journey began in visual communication and advertising, and evolved toward user experience, digital products, and entrepreneurship. Across those disciplines, one question keeps returning: how can we make complex things more useful, human, and understandable?

I founded The YOLO Studio to bring creative thinking and digital execution together. Today, I am also exploring research-led approaches to technology, accessibility, and services in emerging markets.
