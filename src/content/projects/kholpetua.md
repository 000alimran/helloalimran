## Overview

Kholpetua is conceived as a digital home for stories and memories connected to the Sundarbans and the coastal region. It grows from an interest in how places shape the people who come from them.

## The question behind it

How can a digital archive preserve personal and cultural memory without reducing a place to a tourist image or a commercial pitch?

## My relationship to the work

I am developing Kholpetua as a personal creative and storytelling initiative.

## What this page does not claim

The project is presented as evolving, not as a finished archive, published book, established nonprofit, or independently verified cultural institution.
